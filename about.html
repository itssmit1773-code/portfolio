Smit Panchal

I’m interested in how product teams decide what to investigate, prototype, or pilot when the evidence is incomplete.

These independent projects show how I structure that work: define the decision, check the data, compare options, and spell out what a real pilot would need to prove.

The products and datasets are fictional. Each case study explains which results came from the simulation and which decisions still need live validation.
